Sentences

In the SENTENCES-project we investigated how media discussions potentially contribute to individuals’ choices whether or not to take part in cancer screening programs.

News Media

This study investigated how news media discussed cancer screening. We analyzed 5,503 news articles from 2010 to 2022.

Go to the study »

Online Forum

This study investigated how cancer screening is discussed on an online forum. We analyzed one of the largest Dutch forums from 2010 to 2024.

Go to the study »

Social Media

This study investigated how people on social media discuss cancer screening. We analyzed discussions on X/Twitter from 2010 to 2023.

Go to the study »

By using Agent-based modeling on thedataset of messages on X, we were able to model the sentiment about cancer screening discussions.
